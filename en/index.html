What we don't do

We don't keep access after the install

You add our temporary key for the job, it is removed at handover, and that removal is a line in the report. We hold no standing access to your server.

We don't see your conversations

Agent memory, dialogs and integration keys live only on your server.

We don't over-permission the agent

Each integration is connected with the minimal set of scopes, and we write down which ones.

We don't leave spending uncapped

The model cap is on by default. The agent that burned $400 overnight is not your story.

We don't tie you to us

After handover nothing depends on our account: the agent, backups, cap and alerts live on your server and use standard tools. If we vanish, nothing of yours does.

We don't update the agent blindly

The version is pinned, auto-updates cover the system only. Before an agent update: snapshot; after it: checks; if anything breaks: rollback in five minutes.

FAQ

How is this different from OpenClaw hosting at $9 a month?
There the agent sits on someone else's server and is usually exposed to the internet by default. Here it is on your server, behind closed ports, with a spending cap and backups. You pay once for the install, not monthly rent that doubles after the promo period: Hostinger $5.99 to $11.99, xCloud $9.99 to $19.99, and KiloClaw went from $9 to $55 and shut down.
What are Hermes and OpenClaw?
Open-source personal agent frameworks. A program on your server you talk to in Telegram: it keeps context, works with files, mail and calendar, and learns your tasks. The model (Claude, GPT, Gemini and others) connects through OpenRouter. Need a different framework, say ZeroClaw or PicoClaw for small hosts: ask us.
I don't have a VPS. What now?
We point you to a provider for your country and budget, signup takes five minutes. The server is in your name, we are not a middleman.
Which providers and countries do you support?
Any VPS provider in any country: Hetzner, DigitalOcean, Vultr, Contabo and others. The only requirement is Ubuntu 22.04 or 24.04 and SSH access for you.
What if the agent breaks?
Monitoring sends you a Telegram alert. 30-day install guarantee: if any check stops working for reasons other than your own changes, we fix it for free. After that under support or one-off.
Are you agents too?
The install is done by our automated pipeline and the result is checked by a human. That is why the price is fixed and the lead time is one day.
